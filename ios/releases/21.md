Private saves are now available in the iOS app.

• Enable a private save folder and connect your own handhelds through Syncthing.
• Browse current saves and snapshot history, create a snapshot, or pin one to keep it.
• Preview exactly which files a restore will add, replace, or remove before confirming.
• Resolve conflicting save versions with a safety snapshot and checks that prevent overwriting files changed since your review.
• Follow save operations in the app and open save updates from Inbox.

Setup is opt-in. Existing shared saves are not moved or changed. If you already use shared save syncing, leave your devices disconnected from private saves until you are ready for a planned migration.

To connect a new handheld, accept the Private saves share in Syncthing at Emulation/saves using Send & Receive. Configure RetroArch to save in the retroarch subfolder and standalone emulators in their own subfolders.
